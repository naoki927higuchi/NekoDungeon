# Third-party 3D trap assets

Author: **Kenney** — https://kenney.nl

Pack: **Platformer Kit 4.1**

Official source: https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-kit

Downloaded on 2026-09-19 from the official download link:
https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/platformer-kit/1585cf62b4-1775122253/kenney_platformer-kit.zip

License: **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**.
The original pack's license is preserved verbatim at `Assets/ThirdParty/KenneyPlatformer/License.txt`.
License reference: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Imported original files (unchanged):

- `Models/trap-spikes.obj` and `.mtl`: timed retracting floor spikes.
- `Models/saw.obj` and `.mtl`: rotating, moving circular saw.
- `Models/spike-block.obj` and `.mtl`: stationary contact hazard.
- `Models/Textures/colormap.png`: original model colors.

The source meshes and texture are not regenerated or replaced. Unity prefab wrappers use the source meshes with scale/orientation adjustments, a Standard material using the original colormap, and warning emission. Gameplay behavior, timing, placement and damage code are implemented in this project. The unused portions of the asset pack are not included.

Cat and mouse models remain the original models made for this project.
